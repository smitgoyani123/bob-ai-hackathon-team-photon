# Problem Statement

## Who Experiences the Problem

Power grid operations teams — dispatchers, field engineers, operations managers, and maintenance planners — responsible for maintaining electricity supply continuity across distribution and transmission networks.

## The Problem

Power grid equipment (transformers, switches, transmission lines) fails unpredictably. When equipment fails without warning, the consequences cascade immediately:

- Customers lose power
- Critical facilities (hospitals, data centres, emergency services) are affected
- Repair crews are dispatched reactively, often from suboptimal locations
- Resolution time increases when crews lack the right skill or are too far away

Grid operators today typically rely on:
- Fixed maintenance schedules based on age alone
- Manual inspection rounds
- Reactive response after failure has already occurred
- Spreadsheet-based crew tracking with no risk prioritisation

## Current Limitations

| Limitation | Impact |
|---|---|
| No predictive failure signal | Operators cannot act before failure |
| No weather-equipment correlation | Storm exposure ignored until damage occurs |
| No network impact scoring | All failures treated with equal urgency |
| Manual crew dispatch | Crews may be far away or skill-mismatched |
| No decision explainability | Operators cannot explain why an asset is prioritised |
| No scenario modelling | Cannot anticipate risk escalation before it happens |

## Operational Consequences

- **Prolonged outages** — reactive dispatch adds hours to restoration time
- **Wasted field capacity** — crews dispatched to low-risk assets while critical ones wait
- **Customer harm** — critical facilities affected due to undetected high-risk assets
- **Regulatory exposure** — utilities face penalties for prolonged or repeated outages
- **Safety risk** — unplanned failures under storm conditions endanger field personnel

## Why the Solution Matters

Shifting from reactive to predictive operations requires a system that can:

1. Predict which equipment is most likely to fail in the next 7 days
2. Quantify weather exposure as an operational risk
3. Score the grid impact of each asset's potential failure
4. Combine these signals into a single, actionable priority score
5. Explain the decision in terms an operator can act on
6. Recommend the correct maintenance action automatically
7. Assign the nearest qualified crew before failure occurs

GridGuard AI addresses all seven requirements in a single integrated platform, converting raw sensor and incident data into operational crew assignments — with every step traceable and explainable.
